Overview

Ask Long is an AI onchain checker built for Robinhood Chain, with dedicated support for tokens listed under Ask Long By Tag. There is no app to open — everything runs through a reply on X. Tag @AskLongAI with a contract address or an Ask Long By Tag handle and the agent reads the chain directly — contract code, liquidity pools and holder wallets — and replies with a plain-English verdict in seconds.

It does not execute trades and does not hold custody of funds. It only reads and reports what is already on the chain.

Getting Started

1.On X, reply to (or post) with @AskLongAI analyze this CA followed by the Robinhood Chain contract address, or an Ask Long By Tag handle (e.g. $ASKTAG).
2.Ask Long pulls live onchain data — no login, wallet connection or app required.
3.Review the risk score and breakdown in the reply before you decide anything.

What Gets Checked

Contract Risk Scan
Mint functions, ownership status, fee logic and honeypot patterns in the contract code.
Ask Long By Tag Verification
Confirms the tag genuinely resolves to the contract and creator wallet it claims to represent.
Liquidity & Lock Check
LP lock status, pool size and unlock date, read directly from Robinhood Chain.
Holder Distribution
Top wallets and clustered addresses, to flag whale concentration.
Real-Time Alerts
Large sells, LP pulls and ownership changes, flagged as they happen onchain.

Reading the Risk Score

Every scan compresses into a single 0–100 score plus a short written verdict, so no dashboard literacy is required.

70–100Low flagged risk — contract, liquidity and tag checks passed cleanly.
40–69Mixed signals — one or more checks need a closer look.
0–39High flagged risk — critical checks failed or could not be verified.

Tokenomics

Every fee Ask Long generates is split two ways — that is the whole allocation, with nothing set aside outside of it.

80% Buyback & Burn — used to buy $ASK back from the open market and burn it permanently, tightening circulating supply as usage grows.
20% Token Development — funds the AI agent, onchain data infrastructure and new Ask Long By Tag integrations.
